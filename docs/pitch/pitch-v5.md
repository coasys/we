# WE

## Communities grow. Their platforms don't.

You've built something real — a culture, a shared body of decisions and knowledge, a way of working together. But the infrastructure it runs on belongs to someone else, built for their business model and governed by their rules. WE gives communities genuine agency over their environment — infrastructure they control, tools they can shape, built to grow with them.

---

_[6 flip cards — problem front / solution back. Optional 7th.]_

---

**Card 1**

**Front**
_You're stuck renting your own community._

Every group chat, Discord server, or shared workspace lives on someone else's land. One day they change the rules, raise prices, push ads, or shut down — and your entire history, built trust, and hard-earned culture can vanish overnight. You're not a member of the platform. You're a resource on it.

**Back**
In WE, your community owns everything. Data lives in decentralised infrastructure you control — no company can change the rules or pull the plug. Uninstall an experience and your data stays. Move tools and your history comes with you. Everything your community builds remains yours.

---

**Card 2**

**Front**
_Everything is scattered across too many apps._

Your group already juggles Slack + Google Docs + WhatsApp + a voting tool + a calendar + a drive. Every new need means "add another app." Context gets lost, people get exhausted, and half the group stops checking everything. Somewhere in all that noise is a decision nobody saw.

**Back**
WE is one environment where discussion, governance, shared knowledge, and economic coordination all live together and work together — under one identity. A community doesn't bolt on another tool. It evolves its environment.

---

**Card 3**

**Front**
_You can't actually change the tools you use every day._

You're allowed to pick a theme or add a bot, but the real decisions — how votes work, what feedback signals mean, how information surfaces — are locked by the developers. What a "like" means to your community and what it means to theirs are the same button. Your culture gets flattened to fit their tool.

**Back**
In WE, every experience is built from structured descriptions — not locked executable code — that anyone can inspect, adapt, and share. Define what signals mean for your community: methodological quality, urgency, stake — not just likes. Take a governance mechanism from one experience, combine it with another, fork it, publish your version back. The tools fit the community. Not the other way around.

---

**Card 4**

**Front**
_Every community is building in a different language._

As AI makes it trivially easy to generate custom tools and data formats on demand, a hidden problem emerges: every community ends up with its own incompatible version of "a post," "an event," "a resource." The more flexible the generation, the more isolated each community becomes. Flexibility and interoperability end up in opposition.

**Back**
WE uses a shared vocabulary of content building blocks — text, audio, image, video, collection, and more — that stay consistent across every community. AI can generate endlessly varied experiences on top of them, but the underlying data stays readable everywhere. Any community can connect with another's content without any upfront coordination. The alphabet is fixed. The sentences are free.

---

**Card 5**

**Front**
_The tools break when your group grows._

What works great for 20 people becomes chaos at 200. You outgrow the simple tools but switching to something better means losing years of history and starting over. So you suffer in silence with tools that no longer fit — because the cost of change is too high.

**Back**
Because WE separates data from interface, evolving your tools doesn't mean losing your history. Adopt a new governance mechanism, swap in a better decision-making flow, redesign the whole interface — the community's memory stays intact. A group can start with a simple vote and graduate to something more sophisticated when it's ready. The tools change. The community continues.

---

**Card 6**

**Front**
_AI is just a chatbot on the side._

Today's AI can describe changes, suggest features, and write code — but it's structurally outside the product. It can't reshape your interface, modify your governance logic, or actually change how your community works. Locked, opaque software makes AI a very expensive suggestion box.

**Back**
WE is designed to be an environment AI can actively work inside. Experiences have a structure it can read, reason about, and modify. Data uses shared semantics it can interpret. Describe what your community needs — a different way to surface proposals, a fairer decision-making process, a new economic flow — and AI can generate, preview, and deploy it. AI becomes a collaborator inside the environment, not a chatbot beside it.

---

**Card 7 (optional)**

**Front**
_Great ideas stay trapped inside single groups._

One community figures out a brilliant way to run proposals, share resources, or surface important decisions. But that discovery never escapes the group or product it was built inside. Every community keeps reinventing the same wheels.

**Back**
The WE marketplace distributes coordination patterns as reusable, forkable components — governance mechanisms, knowledge interfaces, signalling systems, economic tools. A community that builds something better publishes it. Others adopt and improve it. No central product team decides what everyone gets. The ecosystem learns.

---

## What this adds up to

Most communities today are socially alive but structurally constrained — capable of growing, creating, and self-organising, but unable to evolve the software conditions they live inside.

WE is an attempt to change that. Not by building a better platform, but by giving communities a shared environment where the tools themselves become something that can be owned, shaped, and passed on.

The deeper bet: given the right infrastructure, communities will discover forms of coordination and collective intelligence that no platform team would have designed for them. And those discoveries will spread.

That is what WE is for.
