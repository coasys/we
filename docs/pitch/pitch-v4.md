# WE

## Communities grow. Their platforms don't.

You've built something real — a body of shared decisions, knowledge, and trust. But it lives on infrastructure that belongs to someone else, governed by their rules, built for their business model. WE is a shared environment communities actually own — one that can grow and change as they do.

---

_[6 flip cards — problem front / solution back.]_

---

**Card 1**

**Front**
_Your community's history doesn't belong to you._

Everything your group has built — the decisions, the discussions, the shared knowledge — lives on someone else's servers. When they change their terms, shift their business model, or shut down, you lose it. You're not a member of the platform. You're a resource on it.

**Back**
In WE, the community's data lives in decentralised infrastructure it controls. No company can change the rules or pull the plug. Uninstall an experience — your data stays. Switch tools — your history comes with you. Everything your community builds remains yours.

---

**Card 2**

**Front**
_Coordination has become a full-time job._

Chat over here. Governance over there. Voting somewhere else. Shared documents in a drive no-one checks. Every new need means another tool, another login, another place where context gets lost. By the time something needs a decision, half the group has already missed the conversation.

**Back**
WE is one environment where discussion, governance, shared knowledge, and economic coordination coexist and work together — under one identity. When someone votes, posts, or decides, it lives in the same space as everything else. Your community doesn't need more apps. It needs one environment it can evolve.

---

**Card 3**

**Front**
_You can customise the wallpaper. You can't change the walls._

Platform tools give you settings. But the real decisions — how votes work, what feedback signals mean, how information surfaces — are locked inside the product. You're stuck with whatever the developers thought was universal. Your culture gets flattened to fit their tool.

**Back**
Experiences in WE are structured descriptions, not locked executables. Every layout, governance mechanism, and signal type can be inspected, adapted, and shared. Define what "signal" means for your community — methodological quality, urgency, stake — not just likes. Take a panel from one experience, combine it with another. Fork something from the marketplace, make it yours, publish it back. The tools fit the community, not the other way around.

---

**Card 4**

**Front**
_When you've outgrown your tools, migration costs everything._

What works for 30 people breaks at 300. What serves casual discussion isn't enough for serious governance. But switching platforms means losing years of history, re-onboarding every member, and severing the threads of continuity your community runs on. So groups stay stuck with tools they've outgrown.

**Back**
Because WE separates data from interface, evolving your tools doesn't mean losing your history. Adopt a new governance mechanism, swap in a better decision-making flow, redesign the whole interface — the community's memory stays intact. A group can start with a simple vote and graduate to something more sophisticated when it's ready. The tools change. The community continues.

---

**Card 5**

**Front**
_AI is making custom software easy to generate — and impossible to share._

New AI tools will let communities generate bespoke interfaces and data formats on demand. But if every community generates its own format for "a post" or "an event," the result is thousands of incompatible versions of the same things. Flexibility without shared structure means no community can meaningfully connect with another.

**Back**
WE uses a shared vocabulary of content building blocks — text, audio, image, video, collection, and more — that stay consistent across every community. AI can generate endlessly different experiences on top of them, but the underlying data stays readable everywhere. Any community can query another's content, build on their structures, or remix their work — without any upfront coordination. The alphabet is fixed. The sentences are free.

---

**Card 6**

**Front**
_AI can tell you what to change. It can't do the changing._

AI assistants can describe improvements, write code, and suggest new features — but they're structurally outside the product. They can't reshape an interface, propose and preview a governance change, or adapt the environment to what your community actually needs. Locked, opaque software makes AI a very expensive suggestion box.

**Back**
WE is designed to be an environment AI can actively work inside. Experiences have a structure it can read, reason about, and modify. Data uses shared semantics it can interpret. Describe what your community needs — a different way to surface proposals, a new economic flow, a redesigned decision interface — and AI can generate, preview, and deploy it. AI stops being beside the environment and starts being inside it.

---

**Card 7 (optional)**

**Front**
_Every community reinvents the same wheels._

One DAO figures out a fairer governance structure. One research collective builds a better knowledge interface. One activist network finds a coordination pattern that actually works. But those discoveries stay locked inside the community or product that made them.

**Back**
The WE marketplace distributes coordination patterns as reusable, forkable components — governance mechanisms, knowledge interfaces, economic tools, signalling systems. A community that builds something better publishes it. Others adopt and improve it. No central product team decides what everyone gets. The ecosystem learns.

---

## What this adds up to

Most communities today are socially alive but structurally constrained — capable of growing, creating, and self-organising, but unable to evolve the software conditions they live inside.

WE is an attempt to change that. Not by building a better platform, but by giving communities a shared environment where the tools themselves become something that can be owned, shaped, and passed on.

The deeper bet: given the right infrastructure, communities will discover forms of coordination and collective intelligence that no platform team would have designed for them. And those discoveries will spread.

That is what WE is for.
