# Coasys Pitch Deck — v3

---

## Slide 1

Scattered across the planet are the resources, insights, and creative capacity we need to solve the biggest problems we face as a species.

But right now we're held back by social infrastructure that:

- fragments data (interoperability failures, user lock-in)
- constrains diversity (one-size-fits-all tools, no room for community needs)  
- silos development (duplicated effort, unnecessary competition between compatible tools)
- entrenches incumbents (winner-take-all network effects punish innovation)
- is designed to extract from us, not serve us (private ownership, opaque and dictatorial governance)

Combined, these forces massively constrain our inherent potential for collective intelligence at a time when we need it most.

At Coasys we're working towards a full-stack solution to these problems — designed to scale from small community use to global adoption, and to be self-sustaining soon after launch.

## Slide 2: The pattern

Throughout each layer of our stack a common pattern emerges:

We need systems that enable radical diversity and low-cost experimentation without sacrificing interoperability — and evolutionary dynamics that surface what works without anyone at the top deciding what that is.

**The problem at every layer is the same:** a single choice forced on everyone — one database, one interface, one currency — ends up serving whoever controls the chokepoint.

**The solution at every layer is the same:** a spanning meta-layer that describes the thing without constraining the implementation. Radical diversity at the base. Interoperability at the top. No chokepoint.

- **AD4M** — the data/meaning spanning layer (backend)
- **WE** — the interface/experience spanning layer (frontend)
- **Synergy** — the value/signal spanning layer (economics)

The result is a complex adaptive, self-organising substrate for collective intelligence — one that can rapidly explore new design spaces, upregulate what works for real communities, and let solutions spread freely to all who might benefit from them.

## Slide 3: The meta-crisis, AI, and the urgency

We're not working in a vacuum. The motivation for our work at Coasys is directly tied to dangerous trends in the world around us — and the belief that these can be mitigated with the right social frameworks.

Nature demands coordination. It's necessary for survival, but it can take pathological or healthy forms. AI is a catalyst that is accelerating our path towards one or the other. If we don't cross this paradigm shift soon, we risk collapse into monoculture — where the most powerful systems of control spread to every layer of life, and the diversity needed for long-term resilience is pruned away.

If we can't find forms of innovation that integrate radical diversity into a coherent whole — aligning the incentives of the parts with the interests of the whole — then that diversity will inevitably be consumed by whatever dominant system can manage it.

We need to act. The window is open now.

## Slide 4: AD4M

At the foundation of our stack is the AD4M spanning layer.

Right now app developers are forced into picking one backend/data storage solution — a particular database or protocol — that all users of their tools are then forced to adopt. This design constraint is so ubiquitous that most people aren't even aware an alternative might be possible.

Problems:

- Apps tightly coupled to backend architecture — hard to change if a better solution emerges later
- All communities forced to accept that data architecture even if it's not optimal for their needs
- Data lock-in: hard or impossible to take your data with you when switching apps
- Interoperability absent: incompatible data structures make it hard for apps to talk to each other

AD4M solves this by introducing a new data spanning layer — a semantic graph that can describe any data structure, connected to pluggable "Languages" that map any database or protocol to the graph.

Now app developers don't need to think about which database to use. They build on AD4M, and their communities can freely choose how data is stored. Interoperability is baked in. Data can move between AD4M apps and communities freely, despite variations in underlying data structures. Communities can even switch apps and seamlessly bring their data with them.

Previous attempts at decentralisation each solved part of this: Solid decentralised storage but kept server infrastructure; ActivityPub/Mastodon federated platforms but left admins in control; blockchains centralised consensus and charge for every transaction. AD4M is agent-centric — your node, your data, your keys — with no chokepoint at any layer.

AI agents participate natively: same identity model, same data structures, same neighbourhoods as human participants. Not through an API. As peers.

## Slide 5: WE

At the next level of the stack, WE applies the same pattern to the interface layer.

Right now we're used to rigid, fixed interfaces — a one-size-fits-all approach forced on all users and communities of an app. Developers with an idea for a new tool are forced to build a full siloed app just to bring that idea into the world, and then compete against others for adoption.

Problems:

- Social tools can't be adapted to a community's specific needs — a monoculture is enforced on everyone despite radical diversity of context, even within a single community
- Massive duplication of effort and unnecessary zero-sum competition between developers with compatible innovations
- Interoperability constrained by incompatible UI frameworks and conventions

WE solves this by introducing a UI spanning layer — a meta-language for describing interfaces (JSON schemas), a set of building blocks and design conventions to maximise interoperability, and an open module marketplace where contributions can be freely shared, adopted, and upregulated based on use.

Advantages:

- Framework agnostic (SolidJS default, designed to work with React, Vue, Svelte etc.)
- Safe to share — everything described in JSON
- Easy to inspect, fork, and remix in real-time, at runtime
- Works seamlessly with AI — no-code interface design is native, not a feature

This gives communities the freedom to adapt their tools to their specific needs without fragmenting across silos. And it lets developers focus on the specific things they want to build, without duplicating other work or competing for network effects. Modules can be shared and mixed freely across all WE interfaces.

## Slide 6: Synergy

At the third level of the stack, Synergy applies the same spanning layer logic to the problem of currencies and signals.

All social systems that scale beyond Dunbar's number run into the same fundamental coordination challenge: who can be trusted, for what, how do we exchange value and meaning across radically different contexts, and how do we align the incentives of the parts with the goals of the whole?

Today's tools collapse this into binary signals that compress our values, and currencies disconnected from the cooperative work that generates real value. Up/down votes, emoji reactions, GDP — all losing the signal in the noise.

Synergy provides infrastructure for communities to design their own currency and signalling systems, calibrated to their specific context, with a mechanism to translate those values across community membranes as they choose to connect.

Radical diversity without losing interpretability.

**Synergy Fuel (SF)** is the initial universal currency of the ecosystem — a "wild card" that holds the top of the stack while the richer, community-specific economic structures grow into place. SF funds the infrastructure designed to eventually replace it. Phase 1 token sale completed 2023.

_The full Synergy design is ambitious and in active development. We're building the simpler foundation first._

## Slide 7: Flux — the proof

Flux is our flagship application and the end-to-end proof that the stack works.

A fully distributed social coordination platform — chat, kanban, graph views, posts, audio/video — built entirely on AD4M with no central server. Communities are private, portable, and self-governing. Your data stays yours. Your identity travels with you if you switch apps.

**Live now at [app.fluxsocial.io](https://app.fluxsocial.io)**

We're honest about where we are: an active community is forming, we have engaged audiences on X, and we're at the transition point from technology-ready to community-adopted. Crossing that transition is what this raise is for.
