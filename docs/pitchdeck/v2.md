## Slide 2: The pattern

At Coasys we've applied the same architectural move to three different layers of the social stack:

**The problem at every layer is the same:** systems that force a single choice on everyone — one database, one interface, one currency — trade diversity for convenience and end up serving whoever controls the chokepoint.

**The solution at every layer is the same:** introduce a spanning meta-layer that describes the thing without constraining the implementation. Radical diversity at the base. Interoperability at the top. No chokepoint.

- **AD4M** — the data/meaning spanning layer (backend)
- **WE** — the interface/experience spanning layer (frontend)
- **Synergy** — the value/signal spanning layer (economics)

Together these form a complex adaptive substrate for collective intelligence — one that can explore design space rapidly, upregulate what works for real communities, and let solutions propagate freely to everyone who needs them.

## Slide 3: AD4M — the semantic P2P infrastructure

For thirty years the W3C's Semantic Web vision has been technically correct and practically unused. RDF, SPARQL, SHACL — the right tools for structured, interoperable, meaningful data — never found the right home. They were designed for a web that doesn't recentralise something the moment you decentralise something else.

Solid decentralised storage but kept the server model. ActivityPub/Mastodon decentralised platforms but left administrators in control. Blockchains centralised consensus and charge rent for every transaction. None of them delivered what was actually needed: an unenclosable carrier.

AD4M is where those two halves finally meet.

Every AD4M user runs a sovereign local node. Data lives on your device as a native RDF knowledge graph, queryable with SPARQL, validated with SHACL. Cryptographic identity (DID + Ed25519) is the foundation — not an afterthought. When communities want to share perspectives, they federate peer-to-peer through pluggable "Languages" that can wrap any transport: Holochain DHT by default, but also IPFS, a blockchain, a centralized relay, anything.

**No chokepoint. No server you don't control. No schema imposed from outside.**

Critically: AI agents participate natively — same identity model, same data structures, same neighbourhoods as human participants. Not through an API. As peers.

## Slide 4: WE — the interface spanning layer

The same problem exists one layer up. App developers today build fixed interfaces — one design forced on every community. Innovators who want to contribute a new tool must build a full siloed app and compete for network effects from scratch.

WE solves this by making interfaces composable and portable.

Interfaces are described as JSON schemas — safe to share, easy to inspect, forkable and remixable at runtime. A library of building blocks (components, layouts, widgets) provides the vocabulary. An open module marketplace lets contributions be shared, adopted, and upregulated by use across the entire ecosystem.

**This means:**

- Communities can adapt their tools to their specific needs without fragmenting
- Developers contribute focused modules, not competing monoliths
- The same module can work across any WE-compatible app
- AI can generate and modify interfaces directly — no-code design is native, not a feature

## Slide 6: Synergy — the economic layer

Coordination systems that scale beyond Dunbar's number need a way to signal trust, reward contribution, and align the incentives of the parts with the interests of the whole — across radically different contexts and communities.

Today's tools collapse this into binary reactions and currencies that float free of any real cooperative work. Up/down votes mean nothing. GDP doesn't measure what it's meant to measure.

Synergy provides infrastructure for communities to design their own signalling and currency systems, calibrated to their specific context, with a mechanism to translate value across contexts as communities choose to connect.

**Synergy Fuel (SF)** is the initial universal currency of the ecosystem — designed not as a permanent fixture but as a "joker card" that holds the top of the stack while the richer, community-specific economic structures grow into place. SF funds the infrastructure designed to eventually replace it. Phase 1 token sale completed 2023.

_The full Synergy design is ambitious and in active development. We're building the simpler foundation first and being disciplined about what we claim is done._
